// 01About

I'm a backend engineer based in Bangalore. I care about systems that stay fast and correct under load: concurrency that doesn't surprise you, clean service boundaries, and pipelines you can re-run without fear.

At Expedia Group I build content-domain services and Kotlin/Spring Batch ETL pipelines that ingest third-party travel content at scale. Before that, at Walmart Global Tech, I owned distributed catalog services, led a three-person team on Project ATOM, and built Wally, a ReAct-based AI assistant for merchandising.

Earlier I built order-management APIs at Amdocs and search and test-automation tooling at TCS. These days I'm most interested in getting LLMs to behave reliably inside real backend workflows.
